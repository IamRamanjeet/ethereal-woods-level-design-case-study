# Ethereal Woods — Level Design & Environment Study

**Unreal Engine | Environment Design | Level Design | Player Guidance | Lighting | Real-Time 3D**

Ethereal Woods is an Unreal Engine environment project focused on creating a visually cohesive and navigable woodland space while exploring how environment composition, lighting, geometry and visual hierarchy can influence the player's experience.

This repository documents the **design process, technical implementation, visual development and lessons learned** behind the project.

Rather than presenting only the final environment, this case study focuses on **how the space was constructed, why key decisions were made, and what I learned through the process.**

## Design Objective

The primary objective was to create an environment that felt:

* atmospheric
* cohesive
* visually readable
* believable
* navigable
* visually interesting without overwhelming the player

A key consideration was the relationship between **visual composition and player movement**.

The environment was therefore approached not simply as a collection of assets, but as a space in which composition, lighting, landmarks and environmental structure could influence where the player looks and moves.

---

# Design Process

## 01 — Initial Concept


The initial stage focused on establishing the visual direction and identifying the type of environment I wanted to create.

The main considerations were:

* mood
* scale
* environment identity
* visual landmarks
* potential player paths
* composition

---

## 02 — Blockout & Spatial Structure


The environment was developed from a basic spatial structure before moving toward the final presentation.

This stage was important for understanding:

* scale
* spatial relationships
* sightlines
* movement through the environment
* major points of interest

The objective was to establish the underlying structure before relying on visual detail.

---

## 03 — Environment Composition


Once the spatial structure was established, environmental assets were introduced to develop the composition.

Asset placement was considered in relation to:

* focal points
* negative space
* sightlines
* player direction
* foreground / midground / background separation
* environmental storytelling

---

## 04 — Player Guidance

One of the design considerations of the environment was how the player could understand where to move without relying entirely on explicit UI instructions.

Visual cues can communicate direction through:

* lighting
* contrast
* landmarks
* environmental framing
* pathways
* openings
* composition

This project gave me an opportunity to explore how these elements can work together to create a more natural sense of navigation.

---

## 05 — Lighting


Lighting was used not only for atmosphere but also as a compositional tool.

The lighting development focused on:

* establishing mood
* creating focal areas
* separating important visual elements
* supporting depth
* improving readability
* strengthening the overall visual hierarchy

---

## 06 — Unreal Engine Implementation

The project was developed in Unreal Engine, providing experience with real-time environment construction and presentation.

Areas explored included:

* scene assembly
* materials
* lighting
* asset placement
* environment systems
* real-time rendering
* optimisation

Where relevant, implementation examples are documented separately in this repository.

---

# Optimisation


Real-time environments require a balance between visual quality and performance.

The optimisation process considered areas such as:

* scene complexity
* asset density
* material usage
* lighting cost
* foliage/environment density
* unnecessary detail

The goal was to maintain the intended visual quality while keeping the environment suitable for real-time use.

# What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of how environment art and level design overlap.

The most important lesson was that a visually strong environment is not only about creating attractive assets or scenes.

The arrangement of those elements can affect:

* what the player notices
* where they look
* where they move
* what they perceive as important
* how they understand the space

This project therefore contributed to my development toward **Game / Level Design**, while also building on my existing environment-art and real-time 3D experience.
